KEY FEATURES

Practice recognition, not real exploitation.

The platform reproduces the learning value of phishing attacks while keeping the exercise controlled.

01

Realistic simulations

Simulates realistic phishing emails and common attack patterns such as urgency, impersonation and suspicious requests.

02

Response tracking

Tracks safe training responses such as opening, clicking or reporting a simulated message to identify awareness gaps.

03

Prevention & training

Turns simulation results into practical strategies for phishing prevention, awareness and security training.

04

Safe by design

Uses simulated destinations and never asks participants to submit real passwords, payment details or authentication codes.
